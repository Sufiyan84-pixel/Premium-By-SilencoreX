About Premium

One app that replaces many

Premium is an Android app that puts everything you do on your phone in one place. You can follow friends, chat one-to-one or in groups, post photos and videos, browse the web without ads, write secret notes, generate AI pictures and even run code, without installing a dozen different apps.

It was designed for real-world phones, including budget devices with just 1 GB of RAM, so it stays smooth where heavier apps lag. Messages are end-to-end encrypted, so your private conversations stay private. The whole app is only 24 MB.

Troubleshooting


App not installing?
Turn on "Install unknown apps" for your browser or Files app again, then clear the browser cache and download the APK once more. Make sure the file finished downloading and you have free storage.


App crashing?
Go to Settings → Apps → Premium → Storage → Clear data, then reinstall the APK. Also check that your Android version is 6.0 or newer.


Browser not loading?
Check that your internet works (try another app). Turn off any VPN, then reopen the built-in browser.


AI features not working?
AI image generation needs your own API key. Open the app's Settings, paste your key in the AI section and try again.


How to update?
Download the new APK and install it over the old one. Your data stays safe, no uninstall needed.
